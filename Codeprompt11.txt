# PROMPT 11 — CivicX Doorstep Pickup, Scrap Collection & Local Services

You are a senior full-stack engineer working on my existing CivicX project.

Your task is to inspect the actual repository and implement a new independent module called:

**CivicX Doorstep Pickup & Local Services**

Do not assume anything about the existing code. Read the actual backend, frontend, database schema, authentication, authorization, locality system, file upload system and existing modules before making changes.

The purpose of this module is to connect local residents with nearby businesses and service providers who can collect items from their homes or provide local services.

# 1. Product Vision

Imagine a person has old newspapers, cardboard boxes, plastic bottles, iron items and unused household materials stored at home.

Normally, the person must search for a scrap dealer, contact them and arrange transportation.

CivicX should make this easier.

The customer opens CivicX, selects Doorstep Pickup, uploads photos of the materials, enters a description and submits a pickup request.

Nearby verified scrap dealers receive or discover the request.

They can:
- View the requested materials.
- Check the customer's locality.
- Offer an estimated price.
- Accept or negotiate the request.
- Select a convenient pickup time.
- Visit the customer's house.
- Inspect and weigh the materials.
- Pay the customer.
- Complete the pickup.

The customer does not need to carry the scrap to the shop.

The same concept should eventually support other local pickup and doorstep services.

# 4. Customer Flow — Scrap Pickup

Example:

A customer has old newspapers, iron pieces and cardboard at home.

The customer should be able to:

1. Open CivicX.
2. Select Doorstep Pickup.
3. Select Scrap Collection.
4. Choose the materials available at home.
5. Upload one or more photos.
6. Enter an approximate quantity.
7. Select a pickup address or locality.
8. Add a description.
9. Select a preferred pickup date and time.
10. Submit the request.

Example request:

Category: Scrap Collection

Materials:
- Old newspapers
- Cardboard
- Iron

Approximate quantity:
- Newspapers: 10 kg
- Cardboard: 5 kg
- Iron: 3 kg

Description:
"Old newspapers, cardboard boxes and some iron items are available at my house. Please arrange doorstep pickup."

Location:
Street 9, selected locality.

Pickup preference:
Tomorrow, 10 AM to 1 PM.

These are illustrative details.

# 5. Nearby Scrap Dealer Discovery

When the customer submits a request, eligible nearby scrap dealers should be able to discover it.

The system should match requests using:

- Service category
- Provider's selected service localities
- Delivery/pickup coverage radius
- Provider availability
- Request status

Example:

Customer submits a scrap pickup request in Street 9.

Nearby verified providers:

1. Sri Sai Scrap Traders — 1 km
2. Local Recycling Centre — 2 km
3. Green Scrap Collection — 3 km

These providers should see the request without receiving the customer's full home address.

Only show approximate locality information until a provider is selected and the customer confirms the arrangement.

Only approved providers should be visible to customers and eligible to receive requests.

A provider must only manage their own business profile and service requests.

# 8. Price Offers and Negotiation

This is an important feature.

Scrap dealers should be able to submit an estimated offer.

Example:

Customer request:
- Newspapers: 10 kg
- Cardboard: 5 kg
- Iron: 3 kg

Provider offer:

- Newspapers: ₹12/kg
- Cardboard: ₹5/kg
- Iron: ₹25/kg

Estimated total: ₹220.

These prices are examples only. Do not hardcode them.

The customer should be able to:

- View provider offers.
- Compare offers.
- Accept one offer.
- Reject an offer.
- Request a revised offer.
- Cancel the request before collection.

The provider should be able to:

- Submit an estimated offer.
- Update an offer before customer acceptance.
- Withdraw an offer if it has not been accepted.

Only one provider should be assigned to a request at a time.

Once the customer accepts an offer, other providers must not be allowed to accept the same request.

Use proper database transactions and concurrency protection.

# 9. Final Weighing and Payment

Important real-world situation:

The estimated weight and actual weight may differ.

For example:

Estimated:
- Newspaper: 10 kg

Actual:
- Newspaper: 8.5 kg

The provider must enter the actual weight and final amount after inspecting the materials.

The customer should be shown:

- Original estimated weight.
- Actual measured weight.
- Agreed rate per unit.
- Final amount.
- Any deductions, if applicable.
- Total amount payable.

If the final amount differs from the accepted estimate, require customer approval before completing the transaction.

Payment methods initially:

- Cash
- UPI paid directly to the customer

Do not integrate a payment gateway in this phase.

Payment statuses:

- PENDING
- PAID
- DISPUTED

Store payment confirmation details and timestamps.

Do not mark an order paid merely because the provider clicks a button. Require the appropriate confirmation workflow from both parties.

# 10. Pickup Workflow

Use a clear request lifecycle.

Suggested statuses:

- REQUESTED
- OFFERS_RECEIVED
- OFFER_ACCEPTED
- PICKUP_SCHEDULED
- PROVIDER_ON_THE_WAY
- ARRIVED
- MATERIAL_INSPECTED
- AWAITING_CUSTOMER_APPROVAL
- COLLECTED
- COMPLETED
- CANCELLED
- DISPUTED

The customer should see the latest status.

The provider should be able to update statuses only when allowed by the workflow.

Maintain complete status history.

Do not allow providers to mark requests completed without the required collection and payment confirmations.

# 11. Customer Privacy and Safety

This is critical.

1. Never publicly display the customer's complete home address.
2. Show only approximate locality information to providers before acceptance.
3. Reveal the necessary pickup address only after the customer accepts a provider's offer and confirms the appointment.
4. Never expose a customer's private address to unrelated providers.
5. Allow customers to cancel before pickup under defined rules.
6. Allow customers to report suspicious providers.
7. Allow providers to report suspicious requests.
8. Verify providers before allowing them to accept requests.
9. Maintain records of important actions.
10. Do not expose customer mobile numbers publicly.
11. Do not implement continuous tracking of the customer's home location.
12. Do not permit unauthorized or unsolicited visits.

For customer safety, show the provider's verified business name and relevant contact information before confirming a pickup.
