# CIVICX — PHASE 5: ADMIN DASHBOARD AND COMPLAINT MANAGEMENT

You are a Senior Backend Architect implementing the administrative management system for CivicX.

Repository:
https://github.com/SaiYadav1818/CivicX-

## 1. Context

Inspect and verify Phases 1–4 before implementation.

Expected existing features:
- Authentication and RBAC.
- Citizen complaint submission.
- Complaint image management.
- GPS and location.
- AI-assisted classification.
- Duplicate detection.
- Authority and department mapping.
- Complaint status history.
- Audit logs.

Do not recreate existing functionality.

## 2. Objective

Build a complete admin management backend that allows authorized CivicX administrators to operate the platform.

Administrators must be able to:
- View all complaints.
- Verify complaints.
- Reject invalid complaints.
- Identify duplicate reports.
- Assign complaints to departments.
- Track progress.
- Manage supervisors and department users.
- Review complaint history.
- Generate operational reports.

## 3. Complaint management APIs

Implement or extend:

GET /api/v1/admin/dashboard/summary

GET /api/v1/admin/complaints

GET /api/v1/admin/complaints/{id}

PATCH /api/v1/admin/complaints/{id}/status

POST /api/v1/admin/complaints/{id}/assign

POST /api/v1/admin/complaints/{id}/notes

GET /api/v1/admin/complaints/{id}/history

GET /api/v1/admin/complaints/{id}/duplicates

Support:
- Pagination.
- Sorting.
- Date filters.
- Category filters.
- Status filters.
- Department filters.
- Ward and zone filters.
- Search by complaint reference.

## 4. Department assignment

Implement a controlled assignment workflow.

A complaint may be assigned to:
- A department.
- A department supervisor.
- An authorized department user.

Record:
- Complaint ID.
- Department ID.
- Assigned user.
- Assigned by.
- Assignment date.
- Assignment reason.
- Due date, if configured.
- Assignment history.

Do not assume a government department has accepted a complaint merely because CivicX assigned it internally.

Distinguish:
CIVICX_ASSIGNED from OFFICIALLY_SUBMITTED and OFFICIALLY_ACKNOWLEDGED.

## 5. Internal notes and communication

Allow authorized administrators to add internal notes.

Requirements:
- Store author and timestamp.
- Preserve note history.
- Keep internal notes private.
- Never expose internal notes through public complaint APIs.
- Record important administrative actions in audit logs.

## 6. Dashboard statistics

Implement accurate database-driven statistics:

- Total complaints.
- Newly submitted complaints.
- Awaiting verification.
- Verified complaints.
- Rejected complaints.
- Possible duplicates.
- Assigned complaints.
- In-progress complaints.
- Resolved complaints.
- Complaints by category.
- Complaints by ward.
- Complaints by department.
- Average resolution time, where sufficient data exists.

Do not invent analytics or use sample numbers in production responses.

Clearly distinguish:
- Resolved by CivicX/community action.
- Reported resolved by an authority.
- Independently verified resolved.

## 7. Reports and exports

Implement:

GET /api/v1/admin/reports/complaints

GET /api/v1/admin/reports/complaints/export

Support CSV or Excel export.

Include:
- Complaint reference.
- Category.
- Ward and zone.
- Authority.
- Department.
- Status.
- Submission date.
- Official reference number, if available.
- Resolution date, if available.

Do not export private information unless explicitly authorized.

Prevent spreadsheet formula injection in exported files.

## 8. RBAC

ADMIN:
- Full permitted platform management.

SUPERVISOR:
- Manage complaints within assigned scope.

DEPARTMENT:
- View and update complaints assigned to their department.

CITIZEN:
- No admin access.

Enforce authorization at service and API levels.

Do not grant unrestricted access to every supervisor or department user.

## 9. Audit and accountability

Every important action must record:
- Actor.
- Action.
- Entity.
- Timestamp.
- Previous value where applicable.
- New value where applicable.
- Reason where required.

Never silently overwrite administrative history.
