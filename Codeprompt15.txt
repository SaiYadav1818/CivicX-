# IMPORTANT SCOPE CORRECTION — CIVICX DIGITAL SHOP DISCOVERY PLATFORM

Update the previously provided Prompt 14 implementation requirements.

CivicX is a **local shop discovery and digital business listing platform**, similar in concept to Google Business Profiles.

CivicX is NOT an e-commerce platform.

Our responsibility is to help local businesses display their shops, locations, photos, galleries and the products or services they offer so nearby customers can discover them.

## 1. Core Shop Profile

Every approved shop should have a public digital profile containing:

- Shop name.
- Shop category.
- Shop description.
- Owner-provided address.
- Map location and directions.
- Contact number (only with owner consent).
- Opening hours.
- Shop logo and cover image.
- External website URL (optional).
- Public shop status, if the owner updates it.

## 2. Shop Photo Gallery

Allow shop owners to upload multiple photos.

Gallery categories can include:

- Shop exterior.
- Shop interior.
- Products and collections.
- New arrivals.
- Unique or special products.
- Shop facilities.
- Other shop-related photos.

Customers can view the gallery on the shop's public profile.

Allow owners to add captions, reorder photos, replace images and delete images.

Only authorized shop owners can manage their own galleries.

## 3. Product Showcase — Display Only

Allow shop owners to optionally showcase products they sell.

Product information can include:

- Product name.
- Category.
- Product image.
- Short description.
- Brand (optional).
- Approximate price (optional).
- New arrival label.
- Special or unique item label.

This is a public catalogue for discovery and information only.

There must be NO:
- Shopping cart.
- Checkout.
- Online ordering.
- Online payments.
- Inventory management.
- Stock quantity tracking.
- Delivery management.
- Order processing.

Do not build these functionalities as part of this module.

Product availability and prices must be treated as owner-provided information, not guaranteed live stock.

## 4. Customer Experience

Customers should be able to:

1. Discover nearby shops.
2. Search by shop name.
3. Filter by shop category.
4. Explore shops by locality.
5. View shop profiles.
6. Browse shop photo galleries.
7. Discover showcased products and new arrivals.
8. View optional prices and descriptions.
9. See shop locations on a map.
10. Get directions.
11. Call shops where contact is publicly enabled.
12. Visit the shop's external website.

## 5. Admin Responsibilities

Admins should be able to:

- Verify shop listings.
- Approve or reject shop profiles.
- Moderate shop photos.
- Review reported content.
- Suspend misleading or prohibited listings.
- Manage shop categories.
- Maintain audit logs.

## 6. Technical Requirements

Inspect and reuse the existing CivicX architecture.

- Reuse existing authentication and role permissions.
- Reuse existing shop registration and approval workflows.
- Use existing image/object storage infrastructure.
- Store image references and metadata in MySQL, not large image binaries.
- Validate uploads and image ownership.
- Add pagination for shop listings and galleries.
- Protect shop-owner update operations.
- Do not expose private owner information.
- Add database migrations only for genuinely missing functionality.
- Preserve all existing modules and tests.

## 7. Final Scope

CivicX should act as a digital discovery website for local businesses.

Think of it as:

**Google-style local shop discovery + shop-owned photo gallery + optional product showcase.**

We display what shop owners upload. Customers discover shops, explore their photos and offerings, and contact or visit them directly.

We do not manage the actual commercial transaction between the shop and the customer.

Before implementation, inspect the previous Prompt 14 work and remove or avoid any unnecessary e-commerce or inventory functionality introduced by it. Do not delete existing functionality without checking whether another CivicX module depends on it.
